## Plain English

The challenge asks for a continuous group homomorphism [Jacobian](#/node/Jacobian) Y → Jacobian X attached to every analytic map f : X → Y of compact Riemann surfaces (`Jacobian/Challenge.lean` lines 130–132). `Jacobian.pullback` supplies it (`Jacobian/Solution.lean` lines 174–178).

The body is a wrapper:

```lean
ULiftContinuousAddMonoidHom' (analyticPullbackU f hf)
```

Everything mathematical is in [analyticPullbackU](#/node/TraceDegree.analyticPullbackU): take the trace of holomorphic 1-forms along f, write it as a matrix in the chosen bases, transpose it, and check that the transpose carries the period lattice of Y into the period lattice of X so that it descends to the tori.

## Why the wrapper is needed

`Jacobian X` is defined as `ULift.{u}` of a torus (`Jacobian/Solution.lean` lines 76–79). The torus itself, `ComplexTorus.quotient (Fin (genus X) → ℂ) …`, lives in `Type 0` no matter which universe X lives in, because its carrier is built from `Fin g → ℂ`. But the specification demands `Jacobian X : Type u` for `X : Type u`. The `ULift` fixes the universe; [ULiftContinuousAddMonoidHom'](#/node/ComplexTorus.ULiftContinuousAddMonoidHom') carries homomorphisms across it, with source and target lifted independently (X and Y are in unrelated universes).

So `pullback f hf` applied to a point is: unwrap with `.down`, apply the torus map, wrap again with `ULift.up`.

## How it is used

It is one half of the anti-hack theorem `pushforward_pullback` (`Jacobian/Solution.lean` lines 282–291): [pushforward](#/node/Jacobian.pushforward) composed with `pullback` is multiplication by `ContMDiff.degree f hf`. That identity is what stops pullback and pushforward from being arbitrary homomorphisms, since it forces them to interact through the classical trace relation and through a degree that is a genuine weighted fibre count.
