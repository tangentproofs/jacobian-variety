## Plain English

[BasisAnalyticJacobianU](#/node/AbelJacobi.BasisAnalyticJacobianU) X is the torus ℂ^{g} / Λ_X, where Λ_X is the period lattice: the subgroup of ℂ^{g} generated by the vectors of integrals of the basis forms over closed chart chains. `analyticPushforwardU f hf` is the induced map J(X) → J(Y) for an analytic f : X → Y (`Jacobian/TraceDegree/AnalyticPushforwardU.lean` lines 129–138).

The definition is one line of real content plus three bookkeeping fields: take [pushforwardTraceLiftCLMU](#/node/TraceDegree.pushforwardTraceLiftCLMU), the transpose of f\* in basis coordinates, and push it through the quotient with [mapClm](#/node/ComplexTorus.mapClm).

## The one thing that has to be proved

`mapClm` demands that the ambient map respect the lattices, so the file first proves `pushforwardTraceLift_preserves_latticeU` (lines 103–121).

A generator of Λ_X is a difference `periodVector l₁ − periodVector l₂` where l₁ and l₂ are chart chains in X with the same start P and the same end Q. Together they make a closed loop.

The key input is `Periods.exists_pushforward_chain` (`Jacobian/Periods/ChainPushforward.lean` lines 287–290): for any chart chain l from P to Q in X there is a chart chain l' from f(P) to f(Q) in Y with

```
∫_{l'} η = ∫_l f*η    for every holomorphic 1-form η on Y
```

This is the change-of-variables rule, proved for all forms simultaneously. Apply it to l₁ and l₂. The lemma `pushforwardTraceLiftCLMU_periodVector` (lines 55–86) then says the ambient map sends `periodVector l₁` to `periodVector l₁'`, and likewise for l₂. So the image of the generator is `periodVector l₁' − periodVector l₂'`, a difference of period vectors of two chains in Y with common endpoints f(P) and f(Q), which is a generator of Λ_Y.

## What the header adds

The module docstring explains why this statement is worth a proof at all. It used to be discharged by a shortcut in which the period subgroup was `⊥` — the period pairing was a placeholder returning zero, so every claim of the form "the image lies in the lattice" was vacuous. Against the genuine period lattice it has content, and the header points at the proof below it. The theorem's own docstring repeats the history in one line (`Jacobian/TraceDegree/AnalyticPushforwardU.lean` lines 100–101).

## How it is used

The public [pushforward](#/node/Jacobian.pushforward) is this map with `ULift` on both sides. Composed with [analyticPullbackU](#/node/TraceDegree.analyticPullbackU), it is the subject of the trace identity `pushforward ∘ pullback = deg f • id`, which is the anti-hack check `pushforward_pullback` (`Jacobian/Solution.lean` lines 282–291).
