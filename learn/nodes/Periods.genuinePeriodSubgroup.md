## Plain English

Λ is the subgroup of ℂ^g generated by the [period generators](#/node/Periods.periodGenerators): `AddSubgroup.closure (periodGenerators X)` (`Jacobian/Periods/GenuinePeriodLattice.lean` lines 80–86). That is the whole definition. Taking the subgroup closure is needed because a set of differences is not automatically closed under addition.

Λ is what the Jacobian is a quotient by, so everything depends on it being the right size. Too small and ℂ^g/Λ is not compact. Too large and the quotient collapses and Abel–Jacobi stops being injective. The file history is a reminder that this is where a construction can go wrong: in an earlier state of this repository the period pairing was a placeholder returning 0, so Λ was the trivial subgroup and the "Jacobian" was all of ℂ^g (`Jacobian/Periods/PeriodFullComplexLatticeU.lean` lines 12–20).

## What is proved about Λ

Half of "Λ is a lattice" is proved right here. [periodLattice_span](#/node/Periods.periodLattice_span) (Jacobian/Periods/GenuinePeriodLattice.lean lines 104–153) shows the real span of Λ is all of ℂ^g, viewed as ℝ^{2g}. The argument is genuinely analytic, not combinatorial: if a real-linear functional φ kills every period, write φ(v) = Re Σ cⱼ vⱼ and form ω = Σ cⱼ ωⱼ. Then the integrals of ω have path-independent real part, so ω = 0 by a maximum-principle argument, so all the coefficients cⱼ are zero and φ was zero after all.

The other half, [discreteness](#/node/Periods.genuinePeriodSubgroup_discrete), is proved in a separate file with Hodge theory. Together they make Λ a full lattice, and that is what gets bundled as [genuinePeriodLattice](#/node/Periods.genuinePeriodLattice).

One thing to be careful about when repeating the classical story: the Lean nowhere mentions H₁(X, ℤ). The identification of Λ with the image of first homology is the classical picture that motivates the definition. It is not a theorem in this project.
