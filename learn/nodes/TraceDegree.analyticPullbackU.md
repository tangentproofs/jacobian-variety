## Plain English

[BasisAnalyticJacobianU](#/node/AbelJacobi.BasisAnalyticJacobianU) X is the complex torus ℂ^g / Λ, where g is the genus of X and Λ is the period lattice: the subgroup of ℂ^g generated by the differences of the integral vectors of two chart chains with the same endpoints, which is how a closed chart chain is presented (`Jacobian/Periods/GenuinePeriodLattice.lean` lines 72–86). This is the Jacobian before the universe bookkeeping of `ULift` is applied.

`analyticPullbackU f hf` is the map J(Y) → J(X) induced by an analytic f : X → Y (`Jacobian/TraceDegree/AnalyticPullbackU.lean` lines 99–108). It is built in one step: take the ambient linear map [traceDualPullbackLiftCLMU](#/node/TraceDegree.traceDualPullbackLiftCLMU) on ℂ^{g_Y} → ℂ^{g_X}, and pass it to the quotients with [mapClm](#/node/ComplexTorus.mapClm). The three structure fields (zero, addition, continuity) are read straight off `mapClm` and [mapClm_continuous](#/node/ComplexTorus.mapClm_continuous).

## The one thing that has to be proved

`mapClm` only works if the ambient map respects the lattices. So the file first proves `traceDualPullbackLift_preserves_latticeU` (lines 64–90): every period of Y is sent to a period of X.

The proof splits in two.

If f is constant, the trace of every form is the zero form, so the ambient map is zero, and zero is in every subgroup. This branch is short and completely formal.

If f is not constant, the file builds a branched-cover datum from f itself and hands the work to `traceDualPullbackLift_genuinePeriods` (`Jacobian/TraceDegree/TracePeriods.lean` lines 299–315). A generator of the lattice of Y is the difference of the period vectors of two chart chains l₁, l₂ with the same endpoints P, Q. Along such a chain, the vector of integrals of the traced basis forms differs from Φ(Q) − Φ(P) by a period of X, where Φ is a multiplicity-weighted sum of integrals over the fibre, measured from a fixed base point of X. The two Φ-terms cancel in the difference, leaving a period of X.

## Why this is not a shortcut

The docstring of the lattice theorem records its own history. In an earlier state of the project the period subgroup was `⊥` because the period pairing was a placeholder returning 0. Then every claim of the form "the image lies in the lattice" was trivially true, and the "Jacobian" was all of ℂ^g, not a torus. The current statement is about the genuine lattice, so it has content.

## How it is used

The public [pullback](#/node/Jacobian.pullback) is this map with `ULift` wrapped around both sides. Together with [analyticPushforwardU](#/node/TraceDegree.analyticPushforwardU) it is what makes the trace identity `pushforward ∘ pullback = deg f • id` a real statement about Jacobians.
