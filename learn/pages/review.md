# Review: does the Lean say what the classical names say?

An independent model (Claude, read-only, no build) audited `Jacobian/Solution.lean` and every definition it rests on against the classical theory of compact Riemann surfaces, on 2026-10-30, at `main` @ `6b3d4f4b`. The full report is [learn/JACOBIAN_CLAUDE_AUDIT.md](https://github.com/tangentproofs/jacobian-variety/blob/main/learn/JACOBIAN_CLAUDE_AUDIT.md). This page summarises it.

## Verdict: PASS WITH CAVEATS

**The definitions are the real classical objects, not stand-ins.** That is the part a challenge like this can quietly get wrong, because the challenge's theorems pin the definitions down only loosely, and the comparator checks a definition's *type*, not its body. The audit read the bodies:

| Challenge name | What the Lean defines | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| [genus](#/node/genus) | dim over ℂ of the space of holomorphic 1-forms H⁰(X, Ω¹), with finite-dimensionality **proved** (a Montel–Riesz argument) | the analytic genus |
| [Jacobian](#/node/Jacobian) | ℂ^g modulo the group generated by integrals of a basis of holomorphic 1-forms over closed chains of chart segments; that group is **proved** discrete and of full rank 2g | a genuine compact complex torus of dimension g |
| [Jacobian.ofCurve](#/node/Jacobian.ofCurve) | Q ↦ the class of (∫ from P to Q of ω₁, …, ω_g) | the Abel–Jacobi map |
| [Jacobian.pushforward](#/node/Jacobian.pushforward) | the transpose of pulling back 1-forms along f, descended to the tori | the classical f₊ |
| [Jacobian.pullback](#/node/Jacobian.pullback) | the transpose of the trace (sum over the fibre) of 1-forms along f, descended to the tori; zero for constant f | the classical f* |
| [ContMDiff.degree](#/node/ContMDiff.degree) | 0 for constant f; otherwise the number of preimages of a point counted with ramification multiplicity | the classical degree |

The eleven theorems match the challenge statements exactly (that is what the comparator checks), and each says what its classical name says, with the two caveats in the table below.

| Theorem | Verdict |
|---|---|
| [genus_eq_zero_iff_homeo](#/node/genus_eq_zero_iff_homeo) | faithful, with a caveat: **homeomorphic** to the sphere, not biholomorphic to the Riemann sphere |
| [Jacobian.ofCurve_inj](#/node/Jacobian.ofCurve_inj) | faithful, with a caveat: genus > 0 ⇒ injective only. The converse, and "embedding", are not stated |
| the other nine (holomorphy, ofCurve P P = 0, functoriality, pushforward ∘ pullback = degree ·) | faithful |

## No smuggled hypotheses on the public path

- No `axiom`, `opaque`, `unsafe`, `native_decide`, `implemented_by` or `extern` anywhere in `Jacobian/`.
- The only type class the public signatures lean on, [FiniteDimensionalHolomorphicOneForms](#/node/HolomorphicForms.FiniteDimensionalHolomorphicOneForms), is **proved** for every compact Riemann surface, not assumed.
- Structures that look like assumption bundles (`IsHolomorphic`, `BranchedCoverData`, weighted-fibre conservation) are all **constructed from smoothness** of the map before they are used.
- The earlier `StableChartAt` adapter, which assumed something false, is deleted; its falsity is now a compiled theorem.
- Three `sorry`s remain, all in `Jacobian/Periods/Hurewicz.lean`, used only inside that file. No public declaration depends on them (source reading; see [Trust](#/trust) for the mechanical checks).

## Caveats (what this site will not claim)

1. **The comparator does not cover everything the challenge asks for.** `comparator/jacobian.json` lists the eleven theorems and nine definitions, but not the `T2Space`, `CompactSpace`, `IsManifold` and `LieAddGroup` instances on `Jacobian X`. They are proved in `Solution.lean`, but the harness does not certify them. See [Trust](#/trust).
2. **Vacuous declarations with famous names still compile.** `Jacobian/Periods/PeriodFunctional.lean` and `Jacobian/AbelJacobi/AnalyticOfCurveBasis.lean` are imported but unused by the solution. They contain declarations such as `periodPairing` (the zero map), `riemann_bilinear_identity`, `serre_duality_h1_eq_ℓKD` and `riemannRoch_formula_general` that are trivialities, not the theorems their names suggest. None of them is on the public path, and this site does not cite them. `ref/axiom-audit.md` §0.3 used to claim a "placeholder count in the compiled tree: **0**"; that claim was corrected on 2026-10-30 and the section now lists these declarations by name. The declarations themselves still exist and still compile, and the two files are still in `Jacobian.lean`'s import closure.
3. **Not in the Lean**, so not claimed here: that this genus equals the topological genus (number of handles), except at genus 0; Riemann–Roch; that the Jacobian is an abelian variety (projective, polarized); Abel's theorem for divisors, Jacobi inversion, Torelli; that the lattice is the image of H₁(X, ℤ) (it is the group of periods over closed chart chains, which classically is the same thing); and any link between pushforward/pullback and divisors or the Abel–Jacobi map.
4. **The strict comparator pass is recorded, not re-run here.** The repo reports a pass of the strict configuration on 2026-10-28; `main` has a few later commits. See [Trust](#/trust) for what was re-checked at `6b3d4f4b`.

## What you can say

For every compact, connected, Hausdorff Riemann surface X, the Lean defines the genus as the dimension of the space of holomorphic 1-forms and proves that space finite-dimensional; builds the Jacobian as a genuine compact complex torus of that dimension, ℂ^g modulo a provably discrete, full-rank lattice of periods; defines the Abel–Jacobi map by actual integration and proves it holomorphic, zero at the base point, and injective when the genus is positive; defines the degree of a holomorphic map as the ramification-weighted count of preimages; and proves that pushing forward a pulled-back point multiplies it by the degree. It also proves, from scratch, that genus 0 happens exactly for surfaces homeomorphic to the 2-sphere.

The explanations on this site are drafts (Claude, for Grok Bot), checked against this audit but not yet reviewed by a person.
