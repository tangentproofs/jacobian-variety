# Claude honesty audit: jacobian-variety @ 6b3d4f4b

Date: 2026-10-30. Auditor: Claude (independent, read-only; no build, no `git`, no `lake`).
Mathlib source was **not** present in the checkout (`.lake/` absent), so Mathlib-side claims
rest on my knowledge of `v4.31.0-rc1`, not on reading its files.

**Overall verdict:** PASS WITH CAVEATS

The mathematics is real. Every one of the six definitions the challenge asks for is the
genuine classical object, not a stub dressed up to satisfy the equations: `genus` is
`finrank_ℂ H⁰(X,Ω¹)` with finite-dimensionality *proved* by a complete Montel–Riesz argument;
`Jacobian X` is `ℂ^g` modulo the subgroup generated by honest curve integrals of a basis of
holomorphic 1-forms over closed chart chains, with discreteness and ℝ-spanning both proved
(so a genuine rank-2g lattice and a genuinely compact torus); `ofCurve` is the class of
`(∫_P^Q ω_i)_i`; `pushforward`/`pullback` are the transposes of pullback-of-forms and
trace-of-forms, descended to the tori; `degree` is the ramification-weighted fibre count with
the ramification index pinned to the chart-local analytic order. The hard inputs (Hodge
decomposition of (0,1)-forms, Abel's two-point theorem, period-lattice discreteness, genus-0 ⇒
sphere) are proved from scratch, not admitted. The refuted `StableChartAt` adapter is gone and
its inconsistency is recorded as a compiled theorem. I found no `axiom`, `opaque`, `unsafe`,
`native_decide`, `implemented_by` or `extern` anywhere in `Jacobian/`, and only three `sorry`s
outside the frozen spec, all in `Periods/Hurewicz.lean` and all consumed only inside that file.
The caveats are not about the eleven theorems; they are: (1) four of the seven instances the
challenge demands on `Jacobian X` — `T2Space`, `CompactSpace`, `IsManifold`, `LieAddGroup` —
are **absent from both lists in `comparator/jacobian.json`**, so the comparator does not
certify them at all (they *are* genuinely proved in `Solution.lean`, but by reading, not by the
harness); (2) the compiled tree still contains vacuous declarations carrying famous names
(`periodPairing` is literally the zero map; `riemann_bilinear_identity`,
`serre_duality_h1_eq_ℓKD`, `riemannRoch_formula_general` are trivialities), contradicting
`ref/axiom-audit.md` §0.3's "Placeholder count in the compiled tree: **0**" — none of these is
on the public path, but a site must not cite them; (3) several classical facts an explainer
would naturally reach for are *not* in the Lean (analytic genus = topological genus,
Riemann–Roch, abelian-variety/projectivity, divisor-level description of push/pull); and (4)
the reported strict-comparator pass is dated 2026-10-28, a few commits before this HEAD, and I
could not re-run it.

## The definitions

### `genus`

`genus X := Module.finrank ℂ (HolomorphicOneForm ℂ X)` (`Solution.lean:43`, `:60`).

`HolomorphicOneForm ℂ X` is `Cₛ^ω⟮𝓘(ℂ); ℂ →L[ℂ] ℂ, CotangentSpace ℂ X⟯`
(`HolomorphicForms/Defs.lean:23`) — Mathlib's `ContMDiffSection` with smoothness index
`(⊤ : WithTop ℕ∞)`, which *is* the `ω` of `Challenge.lean`. The bundle is
`Bundle.ContinuousLinearMap (RingHom.id ℂ)` applied to Mathlib's `TangentBundle` and the
trivial line bundle (`HolomorphicForms/CotangentBundle.lean:78`). So "chart compatibility /
cocycle" is Mathlib's vector-bundle structure, not something hand-rolled, and "holomorphic"
is `ω`-smoothness of the section into the total space — over `𝓘(ℂ)` that is ℂ-analyticity.
This is the genuine `H⁰(X, Ω¹)`. Chart readings behave correctly: the transition law
`a_p(w) = τ'(w) · a_q(τ w)` is a theorem (`HolomorphicForms/Finiteness.lean:85`).

Finite-dimensionality is **proved**, not assumed: `finiteDimensional_holomorphicOneForm`
(`Finiteness.lean:323`) is a complete Montel–Riesz argument — finite cover by chart discs,
injectivity of `ω ↦ (a_i(ω)|_{K_i})_i`, a transition bound `‖·‖_L ≤ C‖·‖_K`, Cauchy
estimates giving equicontinuity, Arzelà–Ascoli, then Riesz
(`FiniteDimensional.of_totallyBounded_nhds_zero`). It is registered as an instance at
`Finiteness.lean:372` and re-exposed at `CompactRiemannSurface.lean:788` and
`Solution.lean:34`.

Note the deliberate design point: `genus` is stated as `Module.finrank` directly rather than
as `analyticGenus` (which takes the finiteness instance), so its *value* cannot depend on the
finiteness proof (`Solution.lean:39-42`). Since `finrank` of an infinite-dimensional space is
`0` by Mathlib convention, this matters — and the finiteness theorem closes the hole.

### `Jacobian` and its instances

`Jacobian X := ULift.{u} (ComplexTorus.quotient (Fin (genus X) → ℂ) (periodFullComplexLatticeU X))`
(`Solution.lean:74`). `ComplexTorus.quotient V Λ` is literally `V ⧸ Λ.subgroup`
(`ComplexTorus/Defs.lean:74`). The `ULift` is only a universe move; its transport of the
charted-space / manifold / Lie structures is proved honestly in
`ComplexTorus/ULiftTransport.lean` (`:79`, `:134`, `:365`).

The lattice (`Periods/PeriodFullComplexLatticeU.lean:39` → `GenuinePeriodLatticeDiscrete.lean:80`):

* `genuinePeriodSubgroup X := AddSubgroup.closure (periodGenerators X)`, where a generator is
  `periodVector l₁ − periodVector l₂` for two **chart chains with the same endpoints**
  (`Periods/GenuinePeriodLattice.lean:76`, `:85`), i.e. the integral around the closed loop
  `l₁·l₂⁻¹`. Closed chains are the case `l₂ = []` (`:97`).
* `periodVector X l i := chainIntegral (ω_i) l` with `ω_i` a basis of `H⁰(X,Ω¹)`
  (`GenuinePeriodLattice.lean:60`). `chainIntegral` is a genuine line integral: each segment
  contributes Mathlib's `∫ᶜ x in Path.segment a b, chartForm p ω x`
  (`Periods/ChartChain.lean:167`, `:229`), with `ChartSegment` carrying the proof that the
  segment lies in a ball inside the chart target (`:138`). This is the classical
  `∫ ω(γ t)(γ'(t)) dt` for a piecewise-smooth path, written in charts.
* Spanning over ℝ: **proved** (`GenuinePeriodLattice.lean:110`) — if a real functional killed
  every period, the corresponding form has path-independent real part, so it is `0`.
* Discreteness: **proved** (`GenuinePeriodLatticeDiscrete.lean:27` →
  `Surface/PeriodDiscrete.lean:685`) via a real bilinear form that is `πℤ`-valued on the
  lattice and non-degenerate, itself built on the Hodge decomposition.
* Hence a genuine `IsZLattice ℝ` of rank `2g` (`GenuinePeriodLatticeDiscrete.lean:47`) with a
  compact fundamental domain (`:54`), so the quotient is a compact complex torus.

Charts on the quotient come from `ℂ^g`: at `q` pick a representative `v`, a global lattice
isolation radius `δ`, and use the local section of `mk` on `ball v (δ/4)`
(`ComplexTorus/{Chart.lean:29, ChartedSpace.lean:32}`). Transitions are `ContDiffOn ℂ ω`
(`ComplexTorus/IsManifold.lean:26`). `+`/`−` smooth, giving `LieAddGroup`
(`ComplexTorus/LieAddGroup.lean:17`, `:26`).

**Honest caveat on the lattice.** The Lean lattice is the group generated by periods over
closed *chart chains*, not literally `image(H₁(X,ℤ) → ℂ^g)`. Classically these coincide, and
the Lean proves exactly the properties that pin it down (full rank 2g, discrete). But the
identification with `H₁(X,ℤ)` is **not** formalized — that is what `Periods/Hurewicz.lean`
(polygonal model) was for, and it is unfinished. `IntegralOneCycle X` *is* Mathlib's
`H₁(X;ℤ)` (`Periods/IntegralOneCycle.lean:31`), but the map from it to `ℂ^g` in the tree
(`periodPairing`) is the zero map — see "Smuggled hypotheses and dead placeholders" below.

**Honest caveat on the basis.** `ℂ^g` is the *basis-aligned* model: it uses
`holomorphicOneFormFinBasis = Module.finBasis ℂ (HolomorphicOneForm ℂ X)`, an arbitrary
chosen basis (`HolomorphicForms/BasisAlignedDualEquiv.lean:50`). So `Jacobian X` depends on a
choice of basis up to canonical isomorphism, exactly as the classical `H⁰(X,Ω¹)^*/H₁` does
once you pick coordinates. That is standard, not a cheat, but a site should say "a copy of"
rather than "the" torus if it gets into coordinates.

### `Jacobian.ofCurve`

`ofCurve P Q = [ pathIntegralFunctionalU X P Q ]` where the latter is
`periodVector X (chosenChain X P Q)` (`AbelJacobi/AnalyticOfCurveU.lean:52`;
`PathIntegralFunctionalU.lean:48`) — i.e. the class of `(∫_P^Q ω_1, …, ∫_P^Q ω_g)` along a
`Classical.choice`-selected chart chain from `P` to `Q` (which exists because `X` is
connected). Path independence modulo the lattice is immediate from how the lattice was
generated (`PathIntegralFunctionalU.lean:57`), and the value is the class of the period
vector of *any* chain with those endpoints (`AnalyticOfCurveU.lean:64`). This is the genuine
Abel–Jacobi map. Holomorphy is proved locally with chart primitives (`:82`).

### `pushforward` and `pullback`

* `pushforward f hf` is the descent to the tori of `pushforwardTraceLiftCLMU f hf`, the
  **transpose, in basis coordinates, of pullback of forms `f^*: H⁰(Y,Ω¹) → H⁰(X,Ω¹)`**
  (`TraceDegree/AnalyticPushforwardU.lean:127`). Lattice preservation is proved by pushing
  chains forward with the change of variables `∫_{f∘γ} η = ∫_γ f^*η`
  (`AnalyticPushforwardU.lean:101`, `Periods/ChainPushforward.lean:287`). This is the
  classical `f_*` on Jacobians (dual to `f^*` on forms; equivalently `f_*` on `H₁`).
* `pullback f hf` is the descent of the transpose of **`traceFormsBundled f hf`, the trace
  (norm) of forms along `f`** (`TraceDegree/AnalyticPullbackU.lean:99`). The trace at a
  regular value is literally `Σ_{x ∈ f⁻¹(y)} (df_x^{-1})^* ω_x`
  (`TraceDegree/TraceDefinition.lean:85`), extended across branch values as a holomorphic
  form (`HolomorphicForms/TraceSpec.lean:2710`, `:2777`). This is the classical "pullback via
  trace of forms". Lattice preservation is proved without lifting curves, via a
  multiplicity-weighted fibre potential (`TraceDegree/TracePeriods.lean:298`).
* **Constant `f`:** `traceFormsBundled f hf η = 0` for constant `f`
  (`TraceSpec.lean:2858`), and the inline argument at `AnalyticPullbackU.lean:72-83` shows
  the ambient map is `0` for *every* vector — so `pullback` genuinely is the zero map, as the
  challenge docstring says. But there is **no exported theorem** saying so; the Solution's
  own docstring for `pullback` (`Solution.lean:171`) silently drops the challenge's "Equal to
  the zero map if the map on curves is constant" sentence.
* **Not proved:** nothing connects `pushforward`/`pullback` to divisors or to `ofCurve`. There
  is no lemma of the form `pushforward f hf (ofCurve P Q) = ofCurve (f P) (f Q)` anywhere
  (grep: no match). The maps satisfy the required functoriality and the trace identity, and
  they *are* built from the right classical construction, but their agreement with the
  divisor-level `f_*`/`f^*` is neither stated nor needed.

### `ContMDiff.degree`

`Solution.lean:208`: `0` if `f` is constant; else, if a `RamificationIndexCompatible`
`BranchedCoverData X Y f` exists, the `branchedDegree` of a classically chosen one; else `0`.

* `branchedDegree h = Σ_{x ∈ f⁻¹(y)} h.ramificationIndex x` for any `y`
  (`HolomorphicForms/BranchedCover.lean:152`, `:163`, `:173`) — the weighted fibre count,
  well defined because constancy of the weighted fibre sum is a structure field.
* `RamificationIndexCompatible` forces `ramificationIndex x = mapAnalyticOrderAt f x`
  (`BranchedCover.lean:91`), and `mapAnalyticOrderAt` is the chart-local analytic vanishing
  order of `f(z) − f(p)` (`HolomorphicForms/HolomorphicMap.lean:154`). So the weights are the
  genuine local multiplicities, not free parameters.
* **The "choose" step is honest.** Existence of a compatible datum for any nonconstant
  holomorphic `f` is proved (`Solution.lean:252` → `Blueprint/Sec02/BranchedDegreeFromHolomorphic.lean:61`,
  built from positivity of the order, finite fibres, local `k`-fold structure, finiteness of
  the branch locus, and local conservation of the weighted fibre count); independence of the
  choice is proved (`TraceDegree/AnalyticDegreeU.lean:92`); and the value for *any* compatible
  datum is recorded (`Solution.lean:269`). Both `else 0` branches are proved unreachable in the
  Riemann-surface setting (`Solution.lean:234`, `:252`). `degree = 0` for constant maps is a
  `@[simp]` theorem (`Solution.lean:222`).
* Caveat: `ContMDiff.degree`'s signature carries no `CompactSpace`/`ConnectedSpace` (it must
  match the frozen spec), so as a standalone definition it also applies to manifolds where the
  classical degree is not defined; there it falls back to `0`. In the intended setting it is
  the classical degree.

## Smuggled hypotheses and dead placeholders

**No smuggling on the public path.** Searches over `Jacobian/` found:

* exactly two `class` declarations: `FiniteDimensionalHolomorphicOneForms`
  (`HolomorphicForms/FiniteDimensional.lean:14`) — **proved**, so not an assumption
  (`Finiteness.lean:372`, `Solution.lean:34`) — and `HolomorphicOneFormMontelData`
  (`CompactRiemannSurface.lean:770`), which no public declaration takes as a binder;
* no `axiom`, `opaque`, `unsafe`, `native_decide`, `@[implemented_by]`, `@[extern]` anywhere;
* no global instance that asserts part of the conclusion. The instances in the tree are the
  honest ones (torus structures, the one-point-compactification charts, `ZLattice` facts).
* `IsHolomorphic`, `HasLocalKfoldRamification`, `HasWeightedFiberConservation` are `Prop`
  structures that *look* like assumption bundles, but every one of them is **constructed from
  `ContMDiff` smoothness** before use (`HolomorphicMap.lean:1138`, `:1151`, `:1340`), and
  `Solution.lean:256-258` is where that happens on the public path.
* `BranchedCoverData` bundles "finite fibres" and "weighted fibre sum is constant" as fields —
  which would be a cheat if the datum were assumed. It is not: it is produced from smoothness
  (above).

**`StableChartAt` is gone.** The refuted adapter no longer exists; `ChartStable` is now a plain
predicate and its falsity on any compact connected ℂ-charted space is a compiled theorem
(`Periods/ChartStabilityRefutation.lean:62`, `:101`), with Mathlib's sphere atlas noted as a
concrete counterexample. `Solution.lean:32-33` records the deletion. Remaining mentions are
comments.

**Dead placeholders that still compile (the real honesty problem).** `ref/axiom-audit.md:59`
asserts "Placeholder count in the compiled tree: **0**". That is false. In
`Jacobian/Periods/PeriodFunctional.lean`, which *is* in `Solution.lean`'s import closure (via
`AbelJacobi/AnalyticOfCurveBasis.lean:11` → `Periods/PeriodFunctional.lean:4` →
`HolomorphicForms/HodgeDeRhamRank.lean` → `DeRhamSingular.lean` → `Periods/Hurewicz.lean`):

  **zero map** `H₁(X,ℤ) → H⁰(X,Ω¹)^*` (`PeriodFunctional.lean:94`, `:126`). Consequently
  `periodSubgroup` (`:703`) and `basisAlignedPeriodSubmoduleℤ` (`:758`) are `⊥`.
* `period_homology_invariance` (`:162`) takes `σ = τ` as a hypothesis and is proved by
  `rw [h]` — a tautology under a famous name.
* `wedge_integration_pairing_exists` (`:266`) is proved with `Q := 0`.

Similarly in `Jacobian/AbelJacobi/AnalyticOfCurveBasis.lean` (also imported by `Solution.lean`,
and also unused by it): `serre_vanishing_high_degree` (`:151`) is `h → h`;

**Harness coverage gap.** `comparator/jacobian.json` lists 11 theorems and 9 definitions. The
nine definitions are `genus`, `Jacobian`, `instAddCommGroup`, `instTopologicalSpace`,
`instChartedSpaceForallFinGenusComplex`, `ofCurve`, `pushforward`, `pullback`,
`ContMDiff.degree`. `Jacobian.instT2Space`, `Jacobian.instCompactSpace`, the `IsManifold`
instance and the `LieAddGroup` instance appear in **neither** list, and none of them occurs in
any checked theorem statement (`ContMDiff` needs only `ChartedSpace`, not `IsManifold`). So
the challenge's third anti-hack condition — "the compact complex Lie group instances on
`Jacobian X`" — is outside what the comparator certifies. The instances are real (I read the
proofs), but the correct claim is "the comparator certifies the eleven theorems and the shapes
of nine definitions", not "the comparator certifies the challenge".

Also: definitions are checked as *holes* — the comparator compares the definition's elaborated
**type**, not its body (see `Solution.lean:203-206`, `comparator/README.md`). So the comparator
by construction cannot tell a real `Jacobian` from a fake one; the audit above is what rules
that out.

## Do not say

* ❌ "The Jacobian is an abelian variety / is projective / carries a polarization." Nothing of
  the sort is in the Lean. What is proved is: compact complex torus, Hausdorff, `LieAddGroup`,
  complex dimension `g`.
* ❌ "Riemann–Roch is proved." It is not, in any genus. The declarations named
  `riemannRoch_formula_*`, `serre_duality_*`, `serre_vanishing_*`
  (`AbelJacobi/AnalyticOfCurveBasis.lean:151-230`) are arithmetic trivialities. `ref/plan.md:266`
  marks full Riemann–Roch and Serre duality as deliberately deprioritized.
* ❌ "The Riemann bilinear relations / Hodge positivity are proved." See `PeriodFunctional.lean:290`,
  `:328`, `:406` — vacuous or hypothesis-laden.
* ❌ "Abel–Jacobi is proved in divisor form" / "the Abel–Jacobi theorem". What is proved is the
  **two-point** separation statement (`Surface/Abel.lean:297`) and the resulting injectivity of
  `Q ↦ [∫_P^Q ω]` for `g > 0`. There is no divisor group, no `Div⁰/Pic⁰`, no surjectivity
  (Jacobi inversion), no Abel's criterion for principal divisors.
* ❌ "genus = number of holes" / "the Lean genus is the topological genus." `genus` is
  `dim_ℂ H⁰(X,Ω¹)`. `topologicalGenus` is defined (`Periods/TopologicalGenus.lean:113`) but
  **no theorem relates the two**; `GenusZeroClassification.lean:1482` says outright "No Hodge
  theory, no de Rham cohomology, no topological genus." The only bridge proved is at genus 0.
* ❌ "Genus 0 implies biholomorphic to ℙ¹ / the Riemann sphere, as complex manifolds." The Lean
  gives a **homeomorphism** to `S²` (`genus_eq_zero_iff_homeo`). The intermediate degree-one
  holomorphic map to `ℂ̂` exists (`Surface/GenusZero.lean`), but no biholomorphism is
  constructed. No uniformization theorem.
* ❌ "Torelli", "Jacobi inversion", "Riemann–Hurwitz", "the de Rham theorem",
  "`2g = rank H₁(X,ℤ)`" — none present.
* ❌ "The Jacobian is `ℂ^g` modulo `H₁(X,ℤ)`" stated as a *proved* identification. The lattice
  is the group of integrals over closed chart chains; its identification with the image of
  `H₁(X,ℤ)` is classical but not formalized (and the repo's `H₁`-based `periodPairing` is the
  zero map).
* ❌ "`ofCurve` is an embedding / a closed immersion." Only injectivity is proved.
* ❌ "`ofCurve_inj` shows the Abel–Jacobi map is injective **iff** `g > 0`." Only the forward
  direction is in the Lean (the challenge's own docstring is looser than its lemma).
* ❌ "Pushforward and pullback are the divisor maps." They are the form-theoretic (dual)
  constructions; no divisor-level compatibility, and no compatibility with `ofCurve`, is proved.
* ❌ "A machine-checked proof with zero placeholders." The compiled tree still contains the
  vacuous declarations listed above; `ref/axiom-audit.md:59` is wrong on this point.
* ⚠️ Stale repo facts not to repeat: `README.md:38` says the project is pinned to
  `lean4:v4.28.0` / `mathlib v4.28.0`; the actual pin is `v4.31.0-rc1`
  (`lean-toolchain`, `lakefile.toml:16`). `ref/rkirov-gap-comparison.md` describes a gap list
  that predates the October 2026 repair and no longer matches the tree.

What you *can* say, carefully: for every compact, connected, Hausdorff Riemann surface, this
development defines the genus as the dimension of the space of holomorphic 1-forms (and proves
that space is finite-dimensional); builds the Jacobian as a genuine compact complex torus of
that dimension, quotienting `ℂ^g` by an honest, provably discrete, provably full-rank lattice of
periods of 1-forms over closed loops; defines the Abel–Jacobi map by actual integration and
proves it holomorphic, base-point-vanishing, and injective whenever the genus is positive;
defines degree as the ramification-weighted count of preimages and proves that count is
well defined; and proves that pushing forward a pulled-back point multiplies it by the degree.
It also proves, from scratch, that genus 0 happens exactly for surfaces homeomorphic to the
2-sphere.
